## How to View Your Progress

- **CLI**: Run `/sys engagement` to see your achievement dashboard
- **IDE**: Achievements unlock automatically during development
- **Memory Card**: Check `~/.doplan/memory_card.json` for your achievement list

## Achievement Graphics

Each achievement has a corresponding PNG graphic in the [graphics/](./graphics/) directory. Graphics are named using the achievement ID (e.g., `first_project.png`, `score_1000.png`).

## Earning Achievements

Achievements are automatically checked and awarded when you:
- Complete commands successfully
- Reach score milestones
- Complete projects
- Use DoPlan consistently
- Build your relationship with DoPlan

No manual action is required - just use DoPlan naturally and achievements will unlock!
